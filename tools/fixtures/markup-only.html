PGCB to raise capacity of Mohakhali grid line

The Power Grid Company of Bangladesh (PGCB) will replace the conductors on its 132 kV line along the Mohakhali road, raising the transfer capacity by 180 MW, officials said on Tuesday.

A transmission tower near the Gulshan substation will be rebuilt as part of the work, which is budgeted at Tk 210 crore and scheduled to finish by June 2027.

শুক্রবার সকালে প্রকল্প এলাকা পরিদর্শন করেন পিজিসিবির ব্যবস্থাপনা পরিচালক, যিনি কাজের অগ্রগতিতে সন্তোষ প্রকাশ করেন এবং ঠিকাদারদের সময়মতো কাজ শেষ করার নির্দেশ দেন।

“The line has been running close to its thermal limit since 2024,” a PGCB engineer said, adding that the upgrade would cut load-shedding in the northern parts of the city.

Officials said a block of land beside the substation has already been acquired for the new tower foundations.
