## Findings

1. **non-blocking: an Update moves a story's date ("latest record wins").** `src/editorial/digest.ts:151-157` keeps the newest record per slug and filters on its `publishedAt`; every Update of a live article appends a fresh record dated now (`src/editorial/insights.ts:73-80`, `noteOutcome` line ~115). Reproduce: `node tools/out/qa/update-probe.mjs` with log [A updated 2026-11-02, A published 2026-10-29, B 2026-10-28] → range 25–31 Oct lists only `Story B`; range 1–7 Nov lists `Story A (updated headline) @2026-11-02`. So a typo fix drops a story from the week it was published and shows it as new (with the wrong date in the row) in a later week. Suggested fix: date from the earliest non-dry-run record of the slug, headline/url from the latest. Builder documented it; it does not touch the send path, and the editor reviews the list, so non-blocking, but it should be fixed before editors rely on "This week".
2. **non-blocking: placeholder syntax in data changes the audience, not just the look.** `digest.ts:193,197` writes headline/deck verbatim into the letter. Reproduced: a headline containing `{designation}` makes `skipReason` return "Missing designation" for every contact without that field (`compose.ts missingFields`), and `{name}` is replaced by each recipient's name. `checks.ts` does not flag known placeholder names. No HTML can be injected (fill runs before the escaping renderer; probe 2 shows `rawB:false`, `scriptRaw:false`). Suggested fix: neutralise `{`/`}` in data in `oneLine` for the letter.
3. **non-blocking: composer marks in data are interpreted.** `**bold**` becomes `<strong>`, `[x](https://evil.example.com)` becomes a live link in the preview and the sent letter, and a deck starting `- ` becomes a bullet (`update-probe.mjs` output `"\n\n- looks like a bullet\n[Read the story]…"`). Sources are the desk's own publish log/queue and the site's own feed, so the risk is low. Documented by the builder.
4. **non-blocking: empty feed shown as a failure, and the committed screenshots only show that state.** `DigestDialog.tsx:57,139`: a reachable feed with zero items reads "The site's list could not be read". The `outreach-digest` SETUP (`overnight-shots.mjs`) routes an empty `<rss><channel></channel></rss>`, so all four committed shots show the failure hint and "No deck kept for this story" on every row. The populated state is fine (`tools/out/qa/qa-digest-decks__1366x768__{light,dark}.png`). Suggest a fixture with items for the shot.
5. **non-blocking: the Electron proof could be stronger.** `digest-test.mjs:119-157` spies only `esb:fetch-url`. Spying `esb:outreach-send`, `esb:outreach-verify` and `esb:outreach-inbox` and asserting 0 calls would make "no SMTP" explicit. Renderer-side direct `fetch()` is not observed in Electron mode (the browser suite's `page.route` covers that and saw none).
6. **non-blocking: no error path on Create.** `DigestDialog.tsx:91-99`: `void create()` with try/finally only, so a rejected `createCampaign` gives an unhandled rejection and no message. `createCampaign` is local (state + localStorage + open), so the risk is low.
7. **non-blocking (layout): small windows.** At 1366×768 with ≥5 stories the dialog (706 px) scrolls its body by 17 px. At 1000×700 the single-column layout puts the preview 538 px below the fold. Both stay reachable by scroll, nothing is clipped, and the story list scrolls inside its 320 px cap.

No blocking finding. Not W4's: the 2 D19 editorial failures, the 2 known `test:ui` failures.
